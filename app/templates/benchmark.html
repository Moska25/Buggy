What these columns mean, and what they do not

Recall and precision

recall = defects with at least one detecting check / defects seeded. precision = failing results that are true detections / all failing results. A check that fails on the clean build fails on nearly every build too, so it produces many spurious failures and correctly drags precision down.

FP checks counts checks failing on the clean build. False positives on the scoreboard counts the individual bogus failure reports they generate across the whole matrix - the number a human would actually have to triage.

Mean time-to-detect and flake

Mean TTD runs each suite in registration order and accumulates check duration until the first detecting check fires. It is in-process cost only. It is not a CI wall-clock estimate: there is no process start, no import, no fixture setup, no I/O.

Flake is the share of a suite's checks whose pass/fail outcome was not unanimous across {{ data.run.repeats or 0 }} repeats of the {{ nd_id }} build, the only nondeterministic defect. {{ data.run.repeats or 0 }} repeats is a coarse estimator - a reading of 0.00 means "nothing wobbled in {{ data.run.repeats or 0 }} tries", not "this suite is stable".
